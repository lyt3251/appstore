1Overview

Japan Travel Lens is an iOS app that helps travelers recognize Japanese text on signs, menus, and notices and receive travel guidance. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect your privacy.

By using the app you agree to this policy. Questions: lytbanner105@gmail.com

4How We Use Information

Core functionality — OCR text (and optional image) is sent to our backend server to analyze Japanese text and generate travel guidance.

App improvement — Crash logs and anonymous usage stats help us fix bugs and improve the experience.

Dynamic configuration — Remote Config lets us adjust feature flags without an app update.

We never use your information for advertising, sell it to third parties, or link it to profiles in other services.

8Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe your child has provided personal information, contact us at the address below.

9Data Security

All communication with our backend uses HTTPS encryption. Local data is stored in the iOS app sandbox, protected by iOS file system permissions. The app has no account system and stores no login credentials.
